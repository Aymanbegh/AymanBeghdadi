Biography

Robust perception for autonomous & interactive systems

Robotics · Vision · Embedded Systems

Research spanning academic prototypes, real robotic platforms and industrial immersive systems.

I am a researcher in robotics, computer vision and artificial intelligence. I received my Ph.D. in robotics, computer vision and AI from Université Paris-Saclay, where my doctoral work focused on multisensory interaction between multidimensional, multi-sensor robotic systems and users.

My research focuses on perception methods that remain robust in complex, dynamic and uncontrolled environments. My work combines geometric computer vision, deep learning, semantic scene understanding, graph-based representations and multimodal sensing, with a strong emphasis on real-time deployment.

Alongside academic research, I have industrial experience at Thales on virtual and mixed-reality systems, human factors, cybersickness, multimodal user-state acquisition and computer-vision solutions for robust immersive systems.

Research interests

Scientific focus

My work sits at the intersection of robotic perception, computer vision and embedded intelligence, with the goal of making autonomous or interactive systems more robust, context-aware and deployable.

01

Dynamic Visual SLAM

Geometric and semantic localization and mapping in dynamic indoor/outdoor environments, including depth-aware reasoning and dynamic feature rejection.

02

Scene Understanding & GNNs

Lightweight graph-based scene representations combining semantic and spatial object information for contextual classification and adaptive perception.

03

Robust Computer Vision

Benchmarking and improving object detection under realistic distortions, including context-aware photorealistic image and video degradations.

04

Multimodal Perception

Fusion of visual, inertial, physiological and gaze-related signals to infer user state and support context-aware interaction.

05

Human–Robot Interaction

Multisensory interaction, mixed reality, robot platforms and user-centered perception for interactive and assistive systems.

06

Embedded & Real-Time AI

Integration of perception pipelines on real systems with attention to latency, resource constraints, sensors, testing and software robustness.
